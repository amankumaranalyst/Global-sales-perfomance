# Global Sales Performance Dashboard

Interactive **Power BI** dashboard analyzing global sales, profit, and order performance across markets, regions, and product categories.

---

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes multi-year (2021–2025) global order-level sales data to track overall business performance and uncover profitability trends across markets, regions, customer segments, and product categories — supporting data-driven sales decisions.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

Order-level sales dataset with the following fields:

`Row ID` · `Order ID` · `Order Date` · `Ship Date` · `Ship Mode` · `Customer ID` · `Customer Name` · `Segment` · `City` · `State` · `Country` · `Postal Code` · `Market` · `Region` · `Product ID` · `Category` · `Sub-Category` · `Product Name` · `Sales` · `Quantity` · `Discount` · `Profit` · `Shipping Cost` · `Order Priority`

---

## 🛠️ Approach / Solution

- Modeled and transformed the raw order-level data in Power BI (Power Query + DAX)
- Built dynamic filters for **Year (2021–2025)**, **Country**, and **Region**
- Designed KPI cards and visuals to track sales, profit, and order performance at a glance

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Order, Total Customer, Profit Margin %, Total Quantity
- **Sales by Category** — donut chart (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies)
- **Top 3 Sales by Region** — donut chart (Central, South, Oceania, etc.)
- **Sales by Sub-Category** — donut chart (Phones, Chairs, Copiers, etc.)
- **Sales by Market** — interactive world map
- **Total Profit by Sub-Category** — horizontal bar chart
- **Total Profit by City** — trend chart highlighting top and bottom-performing cities

---

## 🧰 Tech Stack / Key Skills

`Power BI` · `DAX` · `Power Query` · `Data Modeling` · `KPI Reporting` · `Data Visualization`

---

## 📈 Outcome

Delivered a dynamic, filter-driven dashboard that enables quick identification of top-performing markets, regions, product categories, and cities — helping track sales and profit trends at a global scale.
